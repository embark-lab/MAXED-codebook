6 MR21 Measures only (Phase 2)

6.1 ED100k-Plus

The ED100k-Plus is a lab adapted version of the Ed100k. It is a self report measure that assesses current and history of an eating disorder and includes items from the Dieting and Weight History Questionnaire ( variables start with wdqhq in measure), ED History, Eating Disorder Examination-Questionnaire, . It includes questions about binge eating, compensatory behaviors, restriction and other eating disorder symptoms.

6.2 GAD-7

The GAD-7 is a 7 item self report utilized to screen for Generalized Anxiety Disorder and assess severity of symptoms (Spritzer 2006). Each item is rated on a 4-point Likert scale from 0 (not at all) to 3 (nearly every day). The total score is calculated by summing the scores for each item, with higher scores indicating greater severity of anxiety symptoms.

6.4 WHODAS 2.0

The World Health Organization Disability Assessment Schedule 2.0 is a generic assessment instrument for health and disability used across all diseases, including mental, neurological and addictive disorders. It consists of 12 items that are rated on a 5 point Likert scale from 0 (None) to 4 (Extreme or cannot do).
