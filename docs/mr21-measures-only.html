Chapter 6 MR21 Measures only

6.1 ED100k-Plus

The ED100k-Plus is an adapted version of the Ed100k. It is a self report measure that assesses current and history of an eating disorder and includes weight history questions. It includes questions about binge eating, compensatory behaviors, restriction and other eating disorder symptoms.
